Built for the conversation between coach and athlete

Independent assessments sit side by side, turning a lesson into a clear discussion about strengths, development needs, and the next actions that matter.

For coaches

Manage athletes, adapt the skills framework, record assessments, and keep development plans easy to revisit.

For athletes

Complete a self-assessment, compare it with the coach’s view, and keep the next priorities in focus.

For progress

Preserve assessment history and action plans so growth is visible across lessons and seasons.
